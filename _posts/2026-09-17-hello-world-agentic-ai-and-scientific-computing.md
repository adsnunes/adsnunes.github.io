For the past few years, my work has lived at the intersection of two fast-evolving fields: **rigorous scientific computing** (particularly radiative heat transfer, numerical simulations, and physical modeling) and **modern software engineering driven by Agentic AI**.

As the landscape of computation shifts from static code execution to adaptive, multi-step autonomous reasoning systems, an exciting frontier is unfolding. I created this space to document thoughts, technical experiments, architectural blueprints, and reflections along that journey.

---

### The New Frontier: Beyond Static Code

In traditional computational physics and mechanical engineering, our primary currency has always been deterministic precision: discretization schemes, numerical stability criteria, boundary conditions, and convergence checks. Whether solving the Radiative Transfer Equation (RTE) or modeling complex photothermal interactions, every parameter is rooted in physical law.

Meanwhile, the rise of **Agentic AI**—autonomous systems capable of goal decomposition, tool orchestration, self-critique, and persistent memory—is transforming how we approach software development and scientific discovery:

1. **Iterative Problem Formulation:** Agents do not merely complete prompts; they construct hypotheses, design computational experiments, and iterate based on execution feedback.
2. **Deterministic Physics Meets Adaptive Reasoning:** Grounding LLM capabilities into deterministic execution environments (CLI tools, compilers, specialized numerical solvers) creates a hybrid synergy that dramatically accelerates research cycles.
3. **Reproducible Tooling & Knowledge Persistence:** Building harnesses that enable multiple specialized agents to deliberate, verify results against ground truths, and record durable insights.
